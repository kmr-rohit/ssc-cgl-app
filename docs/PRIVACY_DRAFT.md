**CGL Prep** helps you prepare for the SSC CGL exam. This notice explains what we keep about you and why.

## Without an account
Everything stays on your phone or computer (in the browser's storage). Nothing about you is sent to us.

## With an account
When you sign in (with Google or an email code), we keep:
- your **email address**, to sign you in;
- your **study settings**: name, target exam, exam date, study hours;
- your **practice record**: which questions you answered, your choice, whether it was right, and the time taken;
- your **mock tests**, **flashcard reviews** and **saved custom tests**;
- **challenges** you create or take: the paper, the name you choose to show, and your score;
- **mistakes you report** in questions, with any note you write.

We use this only to sync your progress between your devices, to run challenges between friends, and to fix questions.
We look at totals across all students (for example, how many questions were answered today, or which questions most
people get wrong) to improve the content. We do not see or show other students your email.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone except the services that run the app:
Supabase (database and sign-in, servers in Mumbai, India) and Vercel (website hosting). [Add any email or error
monitoring service once chosen.]

## How long we keep it
Until you delete your account. Challenges are removed about 67 days after they are created (60 days open, then a week).

## Your choices
- **Delete your account:** Profile, Account, Delete account. This removes everything above from our servers at once.
- **Get a copy:** Profile, Download backup (what is on this device). [A full server export is planned.]
- **Correct your data:** change your name and settings in Profile at any time.
